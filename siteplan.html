Website Plan

This document presents the plan for Discover Nigeria, a website designed to introduce visitors to Nigerian destinations, culture, food, and travel experiences.

1. Site Name

DN Discover Nigeria

The name Discover Nigeria was chosen because it clearly communicates the purpose of the website. The site will help visitors discover interesting places, foods, traditions, and cultural experiences in Nigeria.

Optional domain: discovernigeria.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Discover Nigeria is to provide useful and interesting information about Nigerian destinations, culture, food, landmarks, and travel experiences.

The website will help tourists, students, Nigerians, and other visitors learn about places to visit and cultural experiences available across Nigeria.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent information that members of the target audience may want to find on the website.

  • What are some interesting places I can visit in Nigeria?
  • What Nigerian foods should I try when visiting the country?
  • What can I learn about Nigerian culture and traditions?
  • How can I contact the website to ask about a destination?

4. Color Scheme

The color scheme is inspired by Nigeria's national colors and natural colors. These colors will provide a consistent visual identity throughout the website.

Green - #0B6E4F

Used for the header, navigation, headings, buttons, and important accents.

Gold - #F4B942

Used for highlights, borders, accents, and call-to-action elements.

Dark - #1F2937

Used for body text to provide strong readability.

Light - #F8F9FA

Used for the main background and content areas.

5. Typography

Montserrat

Montserrat will be used for the website logo, headings, navigation, and buttons.

Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, lists, forms, and general body content.
